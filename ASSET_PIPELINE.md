# EDEN//FALL — production asset pipeline

## Status

The active feature-branch product revision is **0.6.4-authored-art4** on exact **Godot 4.7.1 stable**. The underlying v0.6 runtime/asset ABI remains **0.6.0** where historical IDs, saved metadata and registry contracts require compatibility.

EDEN//FALL uses first-party procedural visual generation and first-party synthesized audio. The historical committed generated pack remains covered by its asset notice. Art4 does not import commercial-game artwork, third-party sprite sheets, recordings, samples, maps or UI.

## Reference policy

External games and public art repositories are research references only.

Commercial design benchmarks:

- Soul Knight — immediate controls, readable hero/weapon identity and compact procedural combat.
- Enter the Gungeon — bullet hierarchy, deliberate dodge timing, room composition, cover and telegraph discipline.
- The Binding of Isaac — contextual reward pools, build identity, silhouette-first enemy language and room cadence.
- Horizon-style post-collapse worlds — environmental storytelling through reclaimed machinery, vegetation and ruined technological scale.

The approved first-party EDEN//FALL reference boards are the direct Art4 production target for silhouette language, pose energy, environment hierarchy, weapon readability and biome richness. Commercial references define quality principles, not copyable pixels or character designs.

Public/open repositories may be inspected for frame conventions, modular asset organization, metadata, testing, palette discipline, performance profiling and provenance practice. No external pixel data enters the first-party generated Art4 path unless it is separately licensed, documented and approved.

## Identity/procedural boundary

Art4 follows one hard rule:

**Authored identity where recognition matters; procedural generation where replayability matters.**

Canonical hero identity is not regenerated by RNG. For Adam, Abel, Cain, Seth and Naamah, the following are stable:

- base silhouette family;
- palette family;
- body proportions;
- head/gear language;
- weapon class/identity;
- identity signature.

Run variation may add bounded wear, relic/status overlays and presentation effects that do not replace the base identity.

Enemy instances remain stochastic but are constrained to approved family modules. Their genome carries explicit authored fields such as:

- `silhouette_anchor`;
- `motion_profile`;
- `material_family`;
- `fx_profile`;
- `weapon_read`.

This prevents a procedural outlaw, cultist, Cherub, biomech or Nephilim from collapsing into the same generic mannequin grammar.

3. `scripts/v8/enemy_genome_director_art4.gd` — motion/material/FX/weapon family direction.

5. `scripts/v8/procedural_sprite_forge_art4.gd` — pose-first Art4 actor construction.
6. `scripts/v8/procedural_world_director_art3.gd` — quiet-floor/landmark compatibility grammar.

Legacy V6/V7 factories remain available because older audits and semantic asset IDs still validate compatibility. They are not allowed to override the final Art4 release binding.

## Current visual ABI

Normal Art4 actor sheets use a compact **48 x 48** frame ABI. Guardian/boss constructions retain **96 x 96** frames where required by inherited contracts.

The runtime addresses four primary Art4 frame meanings per direction:

1. idle/read pose;
2. locomotion pose;
3. attack/recoil/cast pose;
4. dash/special/hurt-response pose.

Eight-direction readability remains part of the inherited sprite contract.

Art4 deliberately prefers a few strong forms over random micro-detail:

- large readable heads;
- compact bodies;
- explicit arm/hand placement;
- oversized readable weapons;
- hard silhouette separation;
- sparse value ramps;
- pose-driven asymmetry;
- family-specific back/halo/horn/wing/tendril language.

The runtime supplements atlas pose changes with breathing/bob, dash echoes, soft contact shadows, muzzle/cast feedback, hit tinting and controlled environmental motion.

## Canonical hero art language

- **Adam:** Eden green/olive, dark-hair survivor read, Genesis Rifle and botanical/Eden cues.
- **Abel:** ivory/gold caster language, halo/focus silhouette and light-oriented accents.
- **Cain:** black/red heavy silhouette, Mark implant language, permanent Mark Cannon, braced recoil stance.
- **Seth:** blue-steel engineer/guardian language, visor/nodes and Watcher Carbine.
- **Naamah:** violet mycelial language, cap/buds/tendrils and Spore Repeater identity.

Hero identity stability is tested across unrelated RNG states; the generated base pixels must remain stable for the same canonical lineage definition.

## Curated procedural enemy art

Standard enemy families remain stochastic within legal bounds. Representative Art4 direction includes:

- feral scavengers: skitter motion, rag/bone materials, short-melee read;
- outlaw gunners: braced stride, scrap/leather materials, long-rifle read;
- raider brutes: heavy lurch, plate/scrap mass and cleaver/heavy read;
- scrap cultists: ritual float, ritual cloth and focus/staff read;
- Cherub drones: wing hover, ivory machine material and halo-gold FX;
- biomech pilgrims: servo stride and ivory-biomech construction;
- Ophanim: wheel/orbit silhouette and radial-eye read;
- Nephilim: bone/flesh or bone/plate families with void/blood/ground-impact accents.

Elite generation may intensify a legal family effect but cannot replace the family with unrelated geometry.

## Environment generation

Five environment grammars preserve the lore progression:

1. **Industrial Eden** — sealed biotechnology, service channels, pressure/containment structures, tanks, failed greenhouses, coolant, roots and breached gene infrastructure.
2. **Ash Wastes** — broken road systems, burned convoys, checkpoints, craters, rebar, exposed foundations and salvage culture.
3. **Temple-Lab** — scientific architecture reorganized into ritual circuitry, archives, sanctums, process machinery and sacred geometry.
4. **Fungal Garden** — mycelial veins, spore basins, fruiting nurseries, root cathedrals and fungal ecology consuming human infrastructure.
5. **Nephilim Ruins** — monoliths, buried gates, rib causeways, colossal remains and devastated megacity-scale biological/machine architecture.

Room composition uses a restrained base surface, a small number of larger macro forms, collision-matched structural cover/landmarks, then localized wear and environmental dressing. Room-sized repeated wallpaper panels are forbidden by the Art3/Art4 design contract.

Art4 room recipes add a stochastic `story_beat`, landmark strength, wall damage, light pockets, prop clusters and hazard pockets. The generated recipe is persisted with the active run so suspend/resume does not visibly reroll an already-entered state.

## Audio

The active first-party audio path inherits the V7 production synthesis model: deterministic generation of source audio assets at build/runtime-contract level, but gameplay playback variation does not consume progression-critical run entropy.

Biome music/ambience and SFX remain original synthesized content. Combat SFX are classified by function rather than falling back to one generic chirp. Warning families distinguish melee/charge, aimed fire, radial/caster and guardian-phase danger.

No external recording or music sample is required by the production generation chain.

## Runtime generation and hitch control

Release validation and runtime startup have separate responsibilities.

Release qualification may generate and inspect the complete required catalog. Normal play should not synchronously rebuild every asset during active combat. Caches and prewarming remain bounded, with expensive generation scheduled outside high-pressure encounter windows where possible.

The active runtime must preserve already-generated recipes and stable canonical hero identity after suspend/resume. Cache trimming may discard reconstructable textures, but it may not mutate active-run semantic state.

## Fail-closed validation

The no-Actions Codespaces/Web qualification path is authoritative for Art4 development testing.

`tools/export_web_no_actions.sh` requires:

- exact Godot 4.7.1;
- clean editor import/parser pass;
- recursive load/instantiation of every GDScript under `scripts` and `tests`;
- bottom-up V7/V8 compile-chain success;
- release binding/version/export-integrity audit;
- Art4 hero/enemy/biome reference audit;
- inherited RC6/RC7/V8 product, presentation, runtime-quality, entropy and streaming gates;
- bounded real main-scene boot;
- non-threaded, non-PWA Web export;
- static WASM/PCK/provenance verification.

Core Art4 pass markers include:

```text
EDEN_ALL_GDSCRIPT_COMPILE_AUDIT=PASS
EDEN_COMPILE_CHAIN=PASS
EDEN_FALL_V8_RELEASE_INTEGRITY_AUDIT=PASS
EDEN_FALL_V8_ART4_REFERENCE_AUDIT=PASS
```

A Web artifact is not considered qualified merely because Godot produced files. `build-info.json` must identify `0.6.4-authored-art4`, the exact full source SHA, the production feature branch, `pwa: false`, `threads: false`, `playable: true`, `qualified: true` and the expected qualification contract.

## Provenance statement

The existing first-party notice remains authoritative for committed generated assets. Art4 preserves the policy that original project geometry/synthesis code produces the active generated images and audio; no third-party commercial artwork, character pixels, maps, samples or recordings are used.

Reference research changes engineering and art-direction rules, not asset ownership. Any future external dependency must be isolated, license-reviewed and recorded before release use.
