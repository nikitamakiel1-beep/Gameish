# EDEN//FALL generated asset pipeline

EDEN//FALL 0.2 ships with an original deterministic visual and audio asset pack generated by `tools/generate_assets.py`.

## Generated visual content

- Five playable-lineage sprite sheets.
- Twenty-five standard-enemy variant sheets across five visual families.
- Five multi-state boss sheets.
- Ten animated weapon sheets.
- One effects atlas with muzzle, impact, dash, heal, shield, critical, pickup, death, portal and boss-phase sequences.
- One animated relic atlas.
- Five biome tilesets.
- One production boot splash.

Humanoid sheets use 48 × 48 frames with eight frames per state and rows for idle, walk, shoot, dash, hurt and death. Boss sheets use 96 × 96 frames with idle, attack, phase and death rows.

## Generated audio content

- Five 24-second biome music loops.
- Five environmental ambience loops.
- Twenty-one combat, traversal, pickup, shop, UI, boss and end-state sound effects.

All audio is synthesized at 22,050 Hz, 16-bit mono from original oscillator, envelope, percussion and deterministic-noise code. No external samples or recordings are included.

## Runtime integration

`scripts/asset_catalog.gd` resolves atlases and animation rows. `scripts/audio_director.gd` controls looping music, ambience, pooled SFX playback and event throttling. `scripts/game_runtime.gd` maps gameplay state to visual animation and audio cues.

## Reproducibility and validation

`.github/workflows/generate-assets.yml` regenerates the package and verifies PNG headers, dimensions, WAV format, duration and exact content counts. `tests/content_validation.gd` verifies that Godot can load every required generated texture and audio stream. The production gate imports the project, executes the content contract and boots the main scene headlessly with Godot 4.6.3.

The generated files are first-party project assets. Their notice is stored in `assets/generated/ASSET_NOTICE.txt`.
